Our story

Frelabs AB grew out of a long friendship. Over many years we have shared the same curiosity about how things work, and the same appreciation for products and services that are simply smart: easy to understand, pleasant to use and built to last.

The company is our way of turning that shared interest into something practical. We explore inventive ideas, test the ones that seem genuinely useful, and develop the best of them into products and services.

What we care about

Making everyday easier. The best ideas are often small ones that remove a bit of friction from ordinary life. We look for the places where a better tool, a clearer service or a simpler routine makes a real difference.

Smart and well designed. We do not think usefulness and good design are a trade-off. Smart solutions should also look and feel good, and the care put into the details is usually what people remember.

Respect for the environment. We prefer natural, durable materials and things that are made to be kept rather than replaced. Choosing well at the start is the simplest way to reduce waste later.

What we are working on

Exploring ideas. Frelabs is a place to try inventive concepts in a small, careful way, before deciding which are worth building out as products or services.

Vaggaro. Vaggaro is one example of what we value: a digital marketing business that shows that smart, practical solutions can be combined with a clean and attractive design.

Sheepskin. Our latest step is the introduction of sheepskin products. Sheepskin is a natural, long-lasting and comfortable material, which fits both our interest in everyday usefulness and our appreciation for the environment.
